The reason you cannot access the sites is likely that your computer doesn't know that jenkins.minikube.local and gogs.minikube.local should point to your Minikube cluster.

Based on your cluster status, the Ingress IP is 172.18.106.220.

Step 1: Update your Windows Hosts File
You need to map the hostnames to the cluster IP in your local machine's configuration.

Open Notepad as Administrator (Search for Notepad, right-click, and select "Run as administrator").
Open the file: C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts
Add the following lines at the end of the file:
text
172.18.106.220 jenkins.minikube.local
172.18.106.220 gogs.minikube.local
Save the file.
Step 2: Restart Gogs (Optional but Recommended)
Since we updated the 
environment.yaml
 with the new external URL, you should restart the Gogs pods so they pick up the new environment variables:

powershell
kubectl rollout restart deployment gogs-deployment
Step 3: Test Access
Try opening the following in your browser:

Jenkins: http://jenkins.minikube.local/
Gogs: http://gogs.minikube.local/
